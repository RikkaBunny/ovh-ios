App: OVH CP 1.5.0 (18), Bundle ID com.hejingcheng.ovhpocket.

This update synchronizes the client with gokele/ovh v0.4.12 (238 catalog operations). It adds queue editing with a fresh status check and pause-before-save, coupons, support tickets, an official OVH chat entry, order payment status/confirmation, and improved renewal, withdrawal and rescue workflows. It fixes the iOS chat presentation window, contact-change response parsing, and guards ambiguous monitor configuration operations. Availability depends on the user's self-hosted backend version, account region and OVH API permissions.

Business screens use Flutter with native iOS integration. Web content is limited to official OVH chat and remote KVM; the app is not a wrapper of the web console. There are no new analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The app has no paid subscriptions or in-app purchases.
